# MGM Laboratory Design System

Welcome to the Storybook for the MGM Laboratory design system.

## Foundations

- **Tokens** — colors, typography, shadows, radii, motion. (See *Foundations/Tokens*.)
- **Brand** — Logo, Wordmark, ShapeSignature, and the 80-tile Pattern catalog.

## Components

Browse the sidebar to see every component in every variant, state, and surface tone (default / muted / inverse). Toggle the **surface** in the toolbar to see how a component behaves on a dark section.

## Accessibility

The a11y addon runs axe-core on every story. A red badge means there's a violation worth fixing.
